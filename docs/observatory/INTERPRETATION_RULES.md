# DeltaCore Scientific Interpretation Rules

This document establishes normative guidelines and epistemic definitions for interpreting outputs produced by the **DeltaCore Adaptive State Observatory**. All reports, summaries, paper drafts, and code comments generated by or for DeltaCore must adhere strictly to these distinctions.

---

## 1. Epistemic Taxonomy

Every claim or analytical assertion belongs to exactly one of the following epistemic categories:

### 1.1. Mathematical Theorem / Condition
- **Definition**: A deductive truth derived logically from stated mathematical axioms, definitions, and assumptions.
- **Scope**: Universal under the stated assumptions; holds regardless of hardware, random seed, or empirical measurement.
- **Standard Phrasing**:
  - *"If $\eta_t \le \frac{2}{\|k_t\|_2^2}$, then the discrete delta transition $(I - \eta_t k_t k_t^\top)$ is a contractive or non-expansive mapping in the direction of $k_t$."*
  - *"The spectral radius $\rho(A_t) \le 1$ when the step size satisfies the contractive upper bound."*
- **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  - Do not state that an empirical survival run "proves stability" or "proves the theorem." (Empirical survival corroborates conformity; proof is deductive).

### 1.2. Empirical Observation
- **Definition**: A concrete, directly measured fact observed during a specific simulation or benchmark execution.
- **Scope**: Local to the specific environment, hardware, PyTorch version, random seed, sequence length, and hyperparameters.
- **Standard Phrasing**:
  - *"In Run `run-042` with seed 42, `SafeSelfReferential` maintained finite memory states across all 50 sequence steps."*
  - *"Under adversarial keys with $\|k\|=6.0$, unconstrained `SelfReferential` produced non-finite values at step 12."*
- **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  - Do not generalize an observation across unobserved regimes (e.g., claiming *"the model will never blow up"* because it survived 50 steps).

### 1.3. Benchmark Result
- **Definition**: A sample statistic (mean, median, standard deviation, success fraction) aggregated over multiple randomized runs under a pre-declared benchmark task protocol.
- **Scope**: Statistically descriptive of the evaluated sample distribution under the specified task configuration.
- **Standard Phrasing**:
  - *"Across 5 seeds on the target-inversion task, `SafeSelfReferential` exhibited a mean first-passage recovery of $5.6 \pm 4.0$ steps compared to $24.2 \pm 5.7$ steps for `FixedDelta`."*
  - *"The survival rate for `SafeSelfReferential` was 100% (5/5) in the tested stress configuration."*
- **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  - Do not label a benchmark comparison an "unconditional victory" or crown a "winner" without stating the corresponding trade-offs (e.g., higher update energy).

### 1.4. Diagnostic
- **Definition**: An internal quantitative probe or metric computed along the trajectory to reveal intermediate system states or mechanistic behavior.
- **Scope**: Explanatory and investigative; not an end-task utility metric.
- **Standard Phrasing**:
  - *"The minimum stability margin $S_t = 2 - \eta_t \|k_t\|^2$ dropped to $-14.3$ prior to numerical overflow."*
  - *"The dynamics-update norm $\|\Delta C_t\|_F$ peaked at step 27, coinciding with rapid step-size expansion."*
- **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  - Do not treat a diagnostic as a normative goal unless explicitly defined as an optimization objective.

### 1.5. Correlation
- **Definition**: An observed empirical co-variation or association between two measured quantities across runs or time steps.
- **Scope**: Descriptive statistical association only; does **not** imply causality.
- **Standard Phrasing**:
  - *"In Task 2, faster recovery latency was associated with higher observed update energy ($r = -0.74, p < 0.05$)."*
  - *"Key correlation $\rho$ correlated positively with mean retrieval error in the evaluated subset."*
- **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  - *"Higher update energy causes faster recovery."*
  - *"Self-referential dynamics caused the reduction in error."* (Unless established via controlled ablation isolating the causal graph).

### 1.6. Hypothesis
- **Definition**: A falsifiable, testable proposition formulated prior to running an empirical experiment.
- **Scope**: Provisional; must state explicit independent variables, dependent metrics, reference baselines, and falsification conditions.
- **Standard Phrasing**:
  - *"H1: Online co-evolution of controller memory $C_t$ enables faster post-shock recovery than fixed-rate adaptation when distribution shifts occur."*
- **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  - Do not rewrite a hypothesis post hoc to match unexpected empirical data. When an outcome diverges from the hypothesis, report it as *Falsified* or *Qualified*.

## 2. Epistemic Guardrails for Observatory Outputs

1. **No Automated "Winner" Designations**:
   The Observatory must never output strings like `"Model A is better than Model B"` or `"Winner: Model A"`. The report must output multi-objective metrics side-by-side (e.g. error, recovery latency, energy, margin).
2. **Strict Non-Causal Event Attribution**:
   When extracting events (e.g., maximum update, threshold crossing, non-finite step), the Observatory documents temporal ordering (`Step 25: shock occurred; Step 27: update peaked; Step 31: recovery threshold crossed`), never causal assertion (`Step 27 peak caused recovery`).
3. **Missing Data Semantics**:
   Undefined metrics (such as recovery latency for a model that never reached the recovery threshold) must be represented as `None` (unreached/missing). They must never be coerced to `0`, `inf`, or `9999` in summary statistics.
4. **Visibility of Failures & Unobserved Post-Failure States**:
   If a model experiences numerical failure (`NaN` or `Inf`), the report must visibly display the failure step, pre-failure diagnostics, and distinguish the observed failure step (`observed = True, finite_state = False`) from unobserved post-failure steps (`observed = False, finite_state = None`). A step after execution terminated must never be described as an observed non-finite state. Under no circumstances may a run with an intermediate non-finite state be reported as a healthy terminal run.
